Obituary Casey-Lee Ballestrini

Casey-Lee Ballestrini, 36, of East Lyme, passed away on March 16, 2021.

Born in New London on March 13, 1985, Casey-Lee was a beloved son, brother, father, and partner who touched the lives of many in the East Lyme community.

Casey-Lee leaves behind his loving parents, Kim Ballestrini and Vinton Augustus Ballestrini; his brother, Jason-Lee Ballestrini; his devoted partner, Tom Longo; and his cherished son, Coda Vinton Riley. He is also survived by DJ Riley, along with many aunts, uncles, and cousins.

Services were held at Thomas L. Neilan & Sons Funeral Homes.
