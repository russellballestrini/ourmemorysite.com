Obituary Jimmy Joel Anderson, Jr.

Jimmy Joel Anderson, Jr., 30, of Waterford, died unexpectedly on Saturday, September 12, 2015.

Born in New London on March 4, 1985, Jimmy was the son of Jimmy Anderson and Robin Lanphear Anderson. He was a graduate of Waterford High School and had been employed as a supervisor for Cross Sound Ferry, where he was a valued member of the team.

Jimmy's presence brought joy and laughter to those around him. He was a devoted father, loving son, and caring brother who cherished time spent with his family and friends.

He is survived by his parents, Jimmy and Robin Anderson; his son, Logan Anderson; his daughter, Lillian Rayne Anderson; and his sister, Megan Anderson. He also leaves behind many loving aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews, cousins, and friends who will forever hold him in their hearts.

A memorial service was held on Saturday, September 19, 2015 at 1:00 p.m. at Thomas L. Neilan & Sons Funeral Home, 12 Ocean Ave., New London.
